What you'll do

  • Support the founder across operations, finance, content, and strategic initiatives.
  • Build, test, and deploy AI workflows, automations, agents, and bots — we live in the Claude / n8n / Zapier ecosystem.
  • Help manage day-to-day ops and finance — vendors, invoicing, basic books, internal coordination.
  • Help strategize and execute the founder's personal content — including being on shoots.
  • Run research projects, summarize them, and present findings clearly.
  • Basically: learn fast, then teach me what you learned.

What we're looking for

  • Genuinely curious — about AI, business, marketing, content, everything.
  • Comfortable with operations and finance basics.
  • Strong written communication (you'll summarize a lot).
  • Self-starter energy — we don't want to micromanage.
  • Bias toward action over overthinking.

Bonus points

  • Hands-on experience with Claude, ChatGPT, n8n, Zapier, Make, or any agent / automation tools.
  • Understanding of marketing, performance, or content (any one is fine).
  • Comfortable on or around a camera for shoots.
  • Have built something on the side — a project, a tool, a Notion system, anything.
